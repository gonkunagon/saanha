## Wooden crating

Recommended for fragile or high-value goods such as glassware, ceramics, mirrors, appliances and furniture.

- Crates protect against impact and stacking in transit
- The crate's weight and size count toward shipping cost
- Crating is charged separately

## Cargo insurance

- Covers loss or damage in transit
- Premium is a percentage of the declared goods value
- Must be declared and purchased before shipping

## Claims

Always photograph or film when opening boxes, and notify our staff within the claim period.
