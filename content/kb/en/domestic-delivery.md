Once goods reach our Thai warehouse, you can choose:

- **Pick up at the warehouse** — no onward delivery fee
- **Private courier** — small to medium parcels, nationwide
- **Chartered truck** — bulky items or large volumes
- **Same-day delivery in Bangkok and vicinity**

Domestic delivery is charged separately from China–Thailand freight, based on weight, size and destination.
