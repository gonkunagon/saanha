## Ordering steps

1. **Send your product list** — share links from 1688, Taobao, Tmall or photos via LINE or our [quote form](/en/quote)
2. **Get a quote** — we check stock, price and China domestic shipping, then send a total in THB
3. **Pay for the goods** — transfer baht and we pay the shop in yuan
4. **Goods arrive at our China warehouse** — items are counted and consolidated
5. **Shipped to Thailand** — by truck or sea; shipping is paid when goods arrive in Thailand
6. **Delivered to you** — via domestic courier, or pick up at our warehouse

## Can't find the product?

Send us a photo or description. Our team will find suitable shops and factories and compare prices for you.
