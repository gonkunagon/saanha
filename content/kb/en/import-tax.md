title: Is import tax included in shipping?
description: All-in shipping vs. separate customs declaration
category: shipping
order: 12
---

Goods imported into Thailand are subject to **import duty** (rate depends on the product type) and **7% VAT**.

## All-in (tax included in shipping)

- The per-kg or per-CBM rate already includes import taxes
- Nothing extra to pay, easy cost calculation
- Suits online sellers and general buyers

## Separate declaration (in the importer's name)

- You receive a proper customs declaration and tax documents in your name
- VAT can be claimed as input tax if you are a VAT-registered company
- Suits companies that need documents for their accounts
