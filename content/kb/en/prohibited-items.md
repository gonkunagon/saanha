## Items we don't ship

- Weapons, military goods and replicas
- Narcotics and e-cigarettes
- Counterfeit or brand-infringing goods
- Flammables, explosives and hazardous chemicals
- Live animals and certain plants

## Items requiring a permit

- Food, cosmetics and supplements (Thai FDA)
- Certain electrical appliances (TISI)
- Wireless communication devices (NBTC)
