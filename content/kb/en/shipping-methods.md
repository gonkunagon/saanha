| | Truck | Sea |
|---|---|---|
| Transit time (approx.) | 5–7 days | 12–18 days |
| Price | Higher | Lower |
| Best for | Urgent goods, small items | Bulky items, large volumes |

## Truck

Overland via Laos or Vietnam. Faster — good for goods you want to sell quickly.

## Sea

More economical for heavy or bulky shipments such as furniture or machinery.

> Transit times are estimates and may be delayed around Chinese holidays or by customs inspections.
