## What is supplier payment?

You transfer baht to us, and we pay your Chinese shop, factory or supplier in yuan via Alipay, WeChat Pay or a Chinese bank account.

## Steps

1. Send the recipient's account details (Alipay / WeChat / bank) and the CNY amount
2. We tell you the THB amount to transfer
3. Transfer and send us the slip
4. Receive proof of payment from the China side

> We do not offer a standalone CNY exchange service — payments are only made to Chinese shops / suppliers
