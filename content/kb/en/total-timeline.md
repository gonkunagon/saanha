China–Thailand freight is only one part. The full timeline has several stages:

| Stage | Approx. time |
|---|---|
| Ordering and shop preparation | 1–3 days (made-to-order goods can take much longer) |
| Shop ships to our China warehouse | 1–4 days |
| China → Thailand freight | Truck 5–7 days / Sea 12–18 days |
| Sorting and delivery in Thailand | 1–3 days |

**Total (approx.):** truck 8–17 days, sea 15–28 days

## Common causes of delay

- Chinese holidays, especially Chinese New Year, when warehouses and shops close
- Shops running out of stock or needing to produce more
- Random customs inspections
