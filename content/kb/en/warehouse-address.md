If you order from 1688 or Taobao yourself, have the shop ship to **our warehouse in China**. We consolidate your goods and ship them to Thailand.

## Steps

1. Ask our staff on LINE for your **customer code**
2. Copy the China warehouse address we send you into the shop's delivery address
3. Add your **customer code** after the recipient name so the warehouse can identify your parcels
4. Send us the **Chinese tracking number** for every order

> Parcels without a customer code or tracking number may not be identifiable and can be delayed.
