## 1. Data we collect

- **Contact details:** name, phone number, LINE ID, email
- **Order and delivery details:** product lists, product links, delivery address
- **Payment details:** transfer slips, and Chinese payee account details you provide
- **Website usage data:** technical data automatically logged by our server, such as IP address

## 2. Why we use it

- To contact you, prepare quotes and provide the services you request
- To purchase goods, make payments on your behalf and ship your goods
- To complete customs formalities as required by law
- To answer questions and resolve service issues

## 3. Legal basis

We process data based on your **consent** (for example when you submit the quote form), as necessary to **perform our service contract**, and to meet **legal obligations** such as customs and tax law.

## 4. Disclosure

We share data only as needed to provide our services, with:

- Shops, suppliers and warehouses in China
- International and domestic Thai shipping companies
- Thai Customs and government authorities as required by law
- Service providers such as LINE (used to notify our team of website quote requests) and our website host

Some data may be transferred to China to purchase and ship goods. We never sell your data.
